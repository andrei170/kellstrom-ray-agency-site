Our Story

Three owners. One name. Seventy-eight years.

Kellstrom-Ray Agency has been a part of Sister Bay since 1948. The agency has passed between just three sets of owners in all that time, and both founding family names remain in the firm name today. That continuity is not an accident. It reflects the way Door County works.

  • 1948 Kermit and Marion Kellstrom founded the agency in Sister Bay, Wisconsin, establishing the region's first independent brokerage in the Northern Door peninsula.
  • 1970s Don and Ray acquired the firm from the Kellstrom family. Both family names were preserved in the agency name, a deliberate nod to the continuity the community trusted.
  • 2007 Heather Collins became the third and current owner in May 2007, taking on the role of Principal Broker. The agency remains independently owned and operated.
  • Today Seven full-time associates. One hundred forty-seven combined years of Door County real estate experience. Offices in Sister Bay and on Washington Island.
